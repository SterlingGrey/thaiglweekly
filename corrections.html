Corrections

When our advice changes, you should be able to see what changed and why. These notes distinguish corrected facts from unresolved questions and do not imply country playback has been tested.

19 October 2026 · When the Love Is Real: revised closing

The original ending expressed hope for a romantic reunion and then predicted that outcome. With Sterling’s approval, the closing now respects both women’s choices without predicting their private lives. The web essay and its downloadable PDF have been updated together, with a dated editorial note. The original publication date remains 10 October. This is an editorial revision, not new reporting.

19 October 2026 · Juliet & Juliet episode count

Previously: our Tracker projected ten episodes, including 31 October and 7 November, from a database count that was not studio-confirmed.

Now: the official GagaOOLala episode 8 page labels it “Finale” and lists 24 October (based on the viewer’s device time). We have changed the Tracker to eight episodes and removed the two unsupported future dates.

Limit: one31’s preview also dates episode 8 to 24 October, but does not explicitly resolve any differences between editions. We have not tested playback or confirmed worldwide access.
