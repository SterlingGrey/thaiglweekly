Shipping and returns

The fine print, in plain words.

Made to order

Nothing here sits in a stockroom. When you order, one piece is printed and shipped by our fulfilment partners.

Where we ship

Many countries. Enter your address at checkout and it confirms whether we can ship to you, before you pay.

What postage costs

It depends on where you live. The checkout shows the exact figure, where your order ships from and the delivery estimate, before you pay.

Customs

If your order is sent from another country, the checkout tells you. Your country may then charge import duty or tax on arrival. Your customs office sets that charge and you pay it. It is not included in the price.

If something is wrong

If the print is faulty or the piece arrives damaged, write to hello@thaiglweekly.com with a photo. It will be replaced or refunded.

Returns

Each piece is made for one order, so we cannot take returns for a change of mind or a wrong size. Please check the size guide before ordering a shirt.

Printing, payment and delivery are handled by our print partner, Fourthwall. Thai GL Weekly never sees your card details.
