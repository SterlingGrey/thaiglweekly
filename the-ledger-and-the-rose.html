Anin Rattanakosin

Senior Corporate Defense Partner · Sterling & Vachira

Anin lets other people fill the silence. By the time she speaks, the person across the table has usually given her more than they meant to. She’s polished, patient and exacting, with a dry wit that surfaces when someone is sharp enough to surprise her.

“Never ask a question you can’t afford to have answered.”

Phattara “Lin”

Junior Associate · Chavalit & Associates

Lin is easy to overlook in her oversized blazer and crooked glasses. She’s happy to let people assume she’s too tired to keep up. While the room moves on, she’s checking the detail everyone else skipped. Once she finds a thread worth pulling, she has a hard time letting go.

“Read the line they hope you’ll skip.”
