Terms

Thai GL Weekly offers a free information service and an optional Full Access plan at $4 a month (monthly only). The free Tracker, official video player and Monday newsletter stay complete and free. Full Access adds the Sunday-evening brief, My Shows (follows, watched marks, calendar feeds), a second Monday essay and extended Top Six, sourced rumour/conflict history, and a private signed-in Tracker view.

What we promise

Every public claim carries a confidence label or a source. When sources disagree we show the conflict instead of quietly picking a side. Corrections run in full. Availability is never invented; “not verified” stays visible.

Full Access

  • $4 per month, billed monthly. Cancel any time. Access continues through the end of the paid period, then ends. No annual plan, no founding rate, no trial.
  • Access fails closed. Any error, outage, mismatch, cancellation or failed payment means no paid content. The free site never depends on the Worker and stays up when Clerk or Stripe is down.
  • Calendar-feed tokens are random, per reader, and deleted when access ends.

What we cannot promise

Studios change dates. Platforms geo-restrict. Uploaders disable embedding. We will be wrong sometimes. When we are, we say so.

Video

Trailers and pilots play in an on-site YouTube iframe, unmodified, with YouTube branding intact. They are not gated on the free site. If embedding is disabled, we fall back to a plain YouTube link.
