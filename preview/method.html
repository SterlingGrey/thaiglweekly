Method

Verification promise: every fact sourced. Confidence labels on every card: aired, confirmed, announced, fan_sourced, unverified.

Conflicts preserved and shown with weight. Corrections lead. Thai productions only. No dating rumors, no relationship speculation.

Date engine runs in Asia/Bangkok. Past episodes never appear as upcoming. Finale = red banner. Series whose finale has aired moves to Wrapped.

Artwork: official YouTube thumbnails or designed placeholders. No open-web posters. No TVDB images.

Free tracker stays free. Paid layer is additive and deferred.
