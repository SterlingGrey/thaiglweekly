My Tracker

Archive scope

Archive scope: Thai productions only. Released scripted series and miniseries are counted in the yearly archives when a women-loving-women romance is central. A continuing series stays under the year it first premiered. Feature films, one-off anthology segments, and substantial sapphic stories inside broader ensemble dramas have their own shelves and do not inflate the standalone-series totals. Small independent web shorts, microdramas, pilots, and incidental moments remain outside this tracker unless reader demand makes one worth adding.
