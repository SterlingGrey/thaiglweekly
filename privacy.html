Privacy

We collect your email address if you subscribe, and nothing else that identifies you. We do not sell it, rent it, or share it.

What we collect

  • Your email address given by you when you subscribe, confirmed by you before we send anything.
  • Whether an issue was opened, and which links were clicked, standard newsletter analytics used to judge whether an issue was useful. Not sold, not shared.
  • The web tracker requires no account. Episode progress you mark is stored in your own browser and never reaches us.

Analytics, without asking you for anything

We do not use Google Analytics. We do not set tracking cookies. Our site measurement does not require tracking cookies. Optional page translation is described separately below.

The site loads Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookieless and stores no personal data. Weekly totals are published on the audience page. Nothing that identifies a person is kept.

Optional page translation

The homepage, Tracker and individual viewing guides offer an optional automatic reading translation. The external translator does not load until you click “Translate this page”. Choosing it connects your browser to GTranslate and Google Translate: public page text is processed for translation, and those services receive normal connection information such as your IP address. Their own privacy policies apply.

The translator uses a googtrans cookie to remember the selected language. This is separate from our cookieless site measurement. Signup forms and newsletter emails remain in English. Automatic translations can contain mistakes; original English is the reference. A translated page does not change a show's viewing rights in your country.

Use “Return to original English” to reload without the translator and clear this site's language-preference cookie. This does not erase information already received by an external service. See GTranslate privacy and Google privacy.

Who processes email

MailerLite stores the list and sends the issues. There is no paid product, so there is no payment processor.

Your choices

Unsubscribe using the link at the foot of any issue. Ask what we hold, or ask us to delete it, at hello@thaiglweekly.com. If you are in the UK or EU those rights are yours under GDPR. We would honour them regardless.
