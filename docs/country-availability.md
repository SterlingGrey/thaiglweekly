# Weekly country availability review

Initial coverage: 12–18 October 2026; Moonshadow, PLS Love, Third Person,
Khom Khlang, Juliet & Juliet and Fairway of Love. Countries: US, UK, PH,
TH, ID, VN, BR. Every existing platform entry has all seven rows.

`status` alone determines the country group. Missing rows mean `not verified`.
`checked` records the dated review described by `check_method` and `note`;
a source-review date NEVER upgrades an unchecked territory to available.
For unverified rows, `source` is a research lead or existing source record,
not proof of territorial rights. No country subtitles are inferred from an
English interface, title heading, or programme's country-of-origin tag.

10 October baseline: 112 rows; two available, 110 not verified, none verified
unavailable. GMMTV's official episode page explicitly establishes Moonshadow's
Thai GMM25 broadcast and oneD replay. This is domestic distribution evidence,
not a playback/subscription/subtitle test. iQIYI lists episodes 1–9 but its
international listing does not establish the seven countries' territorial
rights. PLS Love iQIYI lists 1–5 of 8. WeTV lists both current titles; no
country rights list was exposed. GagaOOLala fetch returned its consent shell.
YouTube trailer fetch failed; existing official schedule records remain leads.
Old reader reports of US Gaga availability/oneD blocking were not promoted to
current verified rows. LINE TV has no established title-specific source.

## Saturday routine

1. Derive next week's titles from the Tracker schedule, not a fixed Top Six.
2. Review each platform's title page and explicit territorial restrictions.
   Record the exact source, review date, method and what was actually checked.
3. Establish each of the seven countries independently. A domestic broadcaster
   announcement establishes domestic distribution only. Never infer worldwide
   YouTube access, or rights from a localized URL. Never use a VPN alone.
4. Use country-specific JustWatch catalogue evidence only with that qualification;
   reader reports remain not verified pending confirmation. If evidence cannot
   establish access or absence, keep `not verified`. Do not invent subtitles.
5. Recheck old definitive claims weekly; downgrade stale/unreproducible evidence
   to not verified and explain it in the note. Keep audit history in the brain.
6. Validate JSON, unique country coverage, status vocabulary, sources and dates;
   run npm test and npm run build. Commit data on main and record the handoff.
