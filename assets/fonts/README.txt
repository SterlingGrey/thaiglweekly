Self-hosted web fonts for thaiglweekly.com.

Chakra Petch (Cadson Demak) and IBM Plex Sans Thai (IBM) are both licensed under the
SIL Open Font License 1.1: https://openfontlicense.org
Files are the Google Fonts woff2 builds, split by script (latin, thai) and downloaded
on 2026-09-04 from fonts.gstatic.com. Chakra Petch v13, IBM Plex Sans Thai v11.

Weights kept: Chakra Petch 400 and 600, IBM Plex Sans Thai 400. Adding a weight means
adding two files here (latin and thai) and an @font-face pair at the top of css/site.css.

Member salon (member page only), added 2026-11-16 on Sterling's word:
Cormorant Garamond 500 (The Cormorant Project Authors), EB Garamond 400 (The EB Garamond
Project Authors) and Jost 400 and 500 (The Jost Project Authors), latin subset, all under
the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Files are the woff2 builds from the Fontsource 5.3.0
packages of the Google Fonts originals (cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/@fontsource/...). The three
licence texts are in licenses/. The public site's stylesheet does not reference them;
premium/portal.mjs writes their @font-face rules.
