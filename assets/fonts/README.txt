Self-hosted web fonts for thaiglweekly.com.

Chakra Petch (Cadson Demak) and IBM Plex Sans Thai (IBM) are both licensed under the
SIL Open Font License 1.1: https://openfontlicense.org
Files are the Google Fonts woff2 builds, split by script (latin, thai) and downloaded
on 2026-09-04 from fonts.gstatic.com. Chakra Petch v13, IBM Plex Sans Thai v11.

Weights kept: Chakra Petch 400 and 600, IBM Plex Sans Thai 400. Adding a weight means
adding two files here (latin and thai) and an @font-face pair at the top of css/site.css.
